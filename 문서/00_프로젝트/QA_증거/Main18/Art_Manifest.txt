LIMITLESS Chapter 2 - Main18 Art Pack
Quest: Main18 "Black Heat" / 검은 열기
Scene: Field_09_ObsidianScar
Display Name: 흑요석 상흔

Art direction: black solidified volcanic rock + obsidian + restrained internal red heat.
This pack intentionally avoids final-volcano, lava-sea, Main20 boss-arena, UI text, BGM, SFX, TTS, Cleanse II, and player/companion art.

Unity placement note:
- Environment PPU / final world placement scale / pivot are NOT fixed in the handoff; determine after Field09 geometry is built.
- Environment source originals in this delivery are 512x512 RGBA working assets.
- Ground assets are filled; prop/overlay assets use transparency.
- Monster sheets follow the specified fixed 1256x1256 / 4x4 / 314x314 / 16-frame layout.
