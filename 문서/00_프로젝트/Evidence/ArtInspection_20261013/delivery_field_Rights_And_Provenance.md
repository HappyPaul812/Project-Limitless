# Pixel Art source, reuse and permission

Field04's new RGBA PNGs in `Ground/`, `Props/`, and `Optional_Story/` are original authored procedural pixel-art drawings generated expressly for LIMITLESS. No bytes, painted pixels or cropped images from ThirdParty or previously approved game sprites were used as bases for these new Field04 files. Existing Field03/Field01 work was consulted only to maintain a compatible top-down pixel-art scale, not copied into these images.

This provenance statement documents this delivered package; it is not an independent legal opinion or an audit of other project's historical art. Final commercial publication should still use the project's normal author/asset register and confirm rights to all other incorporated assets.

The separately shipped Field03 Rock B v2 is **not newly created from scratch**; it reproduces user-approved Field03 v1 pixels exactly (minus orphan pixels and offset). It inherits the original artwork's rights/clearance requirements; no claim of third-party clearance is made here.
