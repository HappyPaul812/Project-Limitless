## Accessibility Design Notes
- Designed as a practical supply/patient transport wagon
- Rear ramp allows wheelchair boarding
- Interior structure visually supports fixing up to 2 wheelchairs
- Bench seating remains available for non-wheelchair party members
- Overall silhouette avoids overly modern or sci-fi styling
